# Acme Corp — Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Annual Allowance

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance based on their scheduled hours.

## 2. Accrual

PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month, starting on the employee's first day. New hires may use accrued PTO after their first 30 days of employment.

## 3. Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any days beyond 5 expire on December 31. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year.

## 4. Requesting Time Off

Employees must submit PTO requests at least two weeks in advance for absences of 3 or more days, and at least 3 business days in advance for shorter absences. Managers must respond to requests within 3 business days.

## 5. Public Holidays

Acme observes 10 public holidays per year. These are separate from the PTO allowance and do not count against an employee's 20 days.
