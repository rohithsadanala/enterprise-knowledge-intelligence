# Acme Corp — Remote Work Policy

**Effective:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All full-time employees

## 1. Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period are eligible for remote work. Contractors and interns are not covered by this policy and should follow the terms in their individual agreements.

## 2. Remote Work Days

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams must agree on at least two common in-office days per week to preserve collaboration. Managers may adjust the split for business needs, but the two-day in-office minimum cannot be waived without written approval from People Operations.

## 3. Equipment

Acme provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time home-office stipend of $500. Employees are responsible for a reliable internet connection with a minimum speed of 50 Mbps. IT support is available for company-owned equipment only.

## 4. Working Hours and Availability

Remote employees must be available during core hours, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local timezone, and must keep their calendar up to date. Work outside core hours is flexible and arranged with the employee's manager.

## 5. Security

Remote work must be performed over the company VPN. Sensitive documents must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage. Any suspected security incident must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.
